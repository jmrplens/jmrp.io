<h2>About This Tool</h2>
<p>
  Content Security Policy (CSP) is a critical security layer that helps detect
  and mitigate certain types of attacks, including Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)
  and data injection attacks. This interactive builder helps you construct
  complex CSP headers by selecting directives and adding trusted sources.
</p>

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>20+ directives</strong> organized by category (Fetch, Navigation,
    Document, etc.)
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Quick presets</strong> for common services like Google Fonts,
    Analytics, YouTube, and CDNs
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Custom domains</strong> — add your own trusted URLs to any directive
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>7 export formats</strong> — ready-to-use config for Nginx, Apache,
    Cloudflare, Vercel, and more
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Report-Only mode</strong> — test your policy without blocking
    resources
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Security assessment</strong> — real-time warnings for insecure
    configurations like <code>'unsafe-inline'</code>
  </li>
</ul>

<h3>How do I use this tool?</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Enable the directives you need for your website</li>
  <li>Use quick presets to add common third-party services</li>
  <li>
    Add custom domains by clicking the <strong>+</strong> button on editable
    directives
  </li>
  <li>Select your server format (Nginx, Apache, Cloudflare, etc.)</li>
  <li>
    Enable <strong>Report-Only mode</strong> to test before enforcing
  </li>
  <li>Copy and add to your server configuration</li>
</ol>

<h3>Privacy</h3>
<p>
  Your security configurations are private. All policy building and formatting
  happens entirely in your browser. No domains, URLs, or policy settings are
  sent to any server.
</p>

<h3>Linux Command Reference</h3>
<p>
  Once you've configured your CSP, you can verify it using terminal tools.
</p>

<h4>How do I check the CSP header with curl?</h4>
<TerminalSession>
  <TerminalSessionCommand>
    curl -I https://jmrp.io | grep -i "content-security-policy"
  </TerminalSessionCommand>
  <TerminalSessionOutput title="CSP Header Output">
    content-security-policy: default-src 'self'; script-src 'self' ...
  </TerminalSessionOutput>
</TerminalSession>

<h4>How do I calculate the hash for an inline script?</h4>
<TerminalSession>
  <TerminalSessionCommand>
    echo -n 'alert("Hello");' | openssl dgst -sha256 -binary | openssl base64
  </TerminalSessionCommand>
  <TerminalSessionOutput title="Base64 Hash">
    qznLcsROU4CHAtWia...
  </TerminalSessionOutput>
</TerminalSession>
