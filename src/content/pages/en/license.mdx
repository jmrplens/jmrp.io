Not everything here carries the same terms. The writing and the cover images are yours to reuse with credit, the portrait is not, and the source code has been open from the start. This page says which is which, and the same terms travel with every page as machine-readable metadata, so a crawler is told what a reader is told.

## Covers

Every cover image on the blog was made for this site, and is published under that same license and on the same terms: copy it, republish it, crop it, build on it, use it commercially, in any medium, provided you credit the author and indicate whether you changed it.

Permission given under this license cannot later be withdrawn from a copy already taken under it. That is the point of it, and it is why the portrait below is deliberately kept out.

## Portrait

The portrait used as this site's avatar is not covered by the license above. It is a photograph of me, the rights to it are mine, and it identifies a person rather than illustrating an article — which is exactly the case where unrestricted reuse does harm rather than good.

It may be used to refer to me: an article about my work, a conference programme, a directory entry, a talk announcement. It may not be used to represent anybody else, or to suggest that I endorse a product or a position.
