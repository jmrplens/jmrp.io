Not everything on this site carries the same terms. The cover images are yours to reuse with credit, the portrait is not, and the source code has been open from the start. This page says which is which — in the same words the structured data on every page gives a machine, so that a reader and a crawler get the same answer.

## Portrait

The portrait used as this site's avatar is not covered by the license above. It is a photograph of me, the rights to it are mine, and it identifies a person rather than illustrating an article — which is exactly the case where unrestricted reuse does harm rather than good.

It may be used to refer to me: an article about my work, a conference programme, a directory entry, a talk announcement. It may not be used to represent anybody else, to suggest that I endorse a product or a position, or as training data for a model that generates likenesses of people.

## Text

Articles, documentation and page copy are all rights reserved. That is simply what copyright already grants them; it is written down here because a page that licenses some things and says nothing about the rest reads as an oversight rather than a decision.

Quoting a passage with attribution needs no permission and is always welcome. Republishing an article in full, translating it, or ingesting the archive into a training corpus is not covered by that — ask, and the answer is usually yes.
